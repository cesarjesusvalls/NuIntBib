   The choice of the proton threshold at 50 MeV is motivated by Fig. 1, where we show the expected number of
selected signal events as a function of the true leading proton kinetic energy for the 1e0p0π and 1eNp0π selections.
The choice of 50 MeV corresponds to the energy where the two curves intersect, so below this value most events are
selected with the 1e0p0π selection, while above this value most events are selected with the 1eNp0π selection.
