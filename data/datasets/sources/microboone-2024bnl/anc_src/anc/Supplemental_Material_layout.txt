Supplemental Material: Measurement of the differential cross section for neutral pion
    production in charged-current muon neutrino interactions on argon with the
                              MicroBooNE detector
The supplemental material contains more details about the results presented in the main text for the νµ CC1π 0 dif-
ferential cross section measurement. The following sections include the ingredients to reproduce the single differential
cross section for each of the five kinematic variables.


                                    I.    EFFICIENCIES : MUON, CHARGED PION KINETIC ENERGY

   Figure 1 shows the reconstruction efficiencies for the νµ CC1π 0 selection as a function of the muon and charged pion
kinetic energies. Phase space cuts used as signal definition in this analysis are placed in regions without significant
slope. The lower efficiency at high charged pion kinetic energy implies that our selection is able to veto charged pions
more effectively in this region.

                               IV.    TOTAL FLUX INTEGRATED CROSS SECTION


  We report the flux-integrated total νµ CC1π 0 cross section for this analysis to be σ = (5.42 ± 1.09) × 10−40 cm2 /
nucleon. This is consistent with the previous MicroBooNE measurement reported in [2].
